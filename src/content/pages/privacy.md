Forged uses self-hosted, privacy-focused analytics to understand how the site is used and which content is useful.

## Analytics

This site uses a self-hosted instance of [Umami](https://umami.is/) to produce aggregate website analytics.

Umami is used to collect information such as:

- Pages viewed
- Referring websites
- Browser and device type
- Operating system
- Country
- Visit and page-view counts

Umami does not use analytics cookies and does not store visitor IP addresses.

Forged does not use advertising trackers, cross-site behavioural tracking, or persistent visitor identifiers.

Browser Do Not Track preferences are respected.

## Purpose

Analytics are used to understand readership, content performance, and how people discover the site.

Analytics data is not used for advertising or profiling.

## Legal basis

Where the analytics data constitutes personal data, processing is based on legitimate interests in operating, understanding, and improving the site.

The analytics configuration is intentionally limited to reduce the amount of data collected.

## Infrastructure

Analytics are hosted on infrastructure controlled by the site owner.

The analytics script and collected analytics data are processed by the self-hosted Umami service at `analytics.damienmurphy.net`.

Cloudflare is used as part of the site's network and delivery infrastructure and may process connection data required to deliver requests.

## Data retention

Analytics data is retained for a limited period and is not kept indefinitely.

## Cookies

Forged does not use cookies for analytics.

Other infrastructure providers may use technologies required for security, network delivery, or essential service operation.

## Your choice

You can disable or re-enable analytics at any time using the control below or the ghost icon in the top navigation. The choice is stored in this browser's local storage and applies only to this browser and device.
